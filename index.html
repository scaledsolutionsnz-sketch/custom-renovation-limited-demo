FAQ

Before you call

Anything else, just ring Umesh on 022 376 2541.

What areas do you work in?

Custom Renovation Limited is based on Pembroke Road in Stratford and works around Taranaki. If your job is further afield, give Umesh a call and ask.

Can you do more than one thing on the same job?

Yes, and that's the point. Customers regularly have the roof painted, the deck roof replaced and the inside repainted in one go, so there's one person to organise instead of several trades.

How do I get a quote?

Call or email with your address, what you'd like done and a few photos if you have them. Umesh will talk it through and arrange to have a look.

Do you take on small jobs?

Reviews include everything from a full kitchen to skirting, plastering and flat pack assembly. If you're not sure whether your job is worth a call, ask anyway.

What happens once I say yes?

You'll agree a start date with Umesh, he'll keep you in the loop as the work goes, and the site gets cleaned up when it's done. Ask about timing and anything else you need to know when you call.
